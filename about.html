A father and son with a love for design

Family owned since 2000, and still small enough that you deal with us directly.

Thank you for visiting Rafla Design. We’d love to get to know you, and we know you’ll decide based on what you know about us. So here it is.

Rafla Design began in 2000 when Joseph Rafla, a civil engineer, designed the homes of his three brothers. His son, Michael, joined what became a successful family practice.

Since then we’ve taken on single and double storey houses, duplexes, townhouses, granny flats and more. No job is too small. Whether it’s a new build or a renovation, we keep budget and realistic, buildable design at the front.

Our tight-knit team means you get our undivided attention and personal service, for a journey that’s as smooth as possible.
