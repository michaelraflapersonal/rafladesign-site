Five clear steps from first conversation to ready to build

You always know what happens next and what it costs. Payment is split in two: 50% to start, 50% when you approve the final plan.

  1. Free initial consultation

    We meet, free and without obligation. Tell us what you want, or work it out with us. If you’re happy to go ahead, a 50% deposit gets us started.

    Free, no obligation

  2. Survey plan

    Already have a survey? Great. If not, use your own surveyor or we’ll arrange one. Their fee goes straight to them; we add nothing on top.

    No markup on surveys

  3. Design consultation

    We draft design options from your brief, present them, and adjust until you’re 100% happy. Once you approve the final plan, the remaining 50% is paid.

    Revisions until you’re happy

  4. Council submission and approval

    We lodge the plans and deal with Council directly. If they ask for minor changes, we make them at no extra cost, all the way to approval.

    Minor Council changes included

  5. Start building

    You have your approval pack and you’re ready to build. We’re happy to oversee the build against the plans and stay on hand for questions.

    Build support available

Ready for step one?

Your first consultation costs nothing and comes with no obligation.
