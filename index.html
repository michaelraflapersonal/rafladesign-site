Why families and builders come back to us

There are plenty of design firms. Here’s what working with a small family practice means for your project.

We’re a family business, so we care

We know what makes a family home, a smart investment and a great design, and we’ll be straight with you about what will and won’t work. No false promises.

You get our undivided attention

The same people stay with you from the first meeting to approval. Your job never lands in the hands of an unfamiliar face.

We deal with Council for you

Going back and forth with Council is rarely easy. We take that on until your job is fully approved and ready to build.

No surprise costs

Fixed pricing, and no extra charge for minor changes to your plan. We work to keep you within budget.

From first chat to approval in five steps

A clear path, with payments split 50/50 and Council handled by us. See the full process.

  1. Free initial consultation

    We meet, free and without obligation.

  2. Survey plan

    Already have a survey? Great.

  3. Design consultation

    We draft design options from your brief, present them, and adjust until you’re 100% happy.

  4. Council submission and approval

    We lodge the plans and deal with Council directly.

  5. Start building

    You have your approval pack and you’re ready to build.
